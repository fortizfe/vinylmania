# Your wishlist

Your library is the "records I own" half of collecting. Your **wishlist** is the
other half: the records you want to buy next.

In Vinylmania the wishlist is not a separate list kept only here. It is your
real **Discogs wantlist**, shown inside Vinylmania. Anything you do to it here —
adding a record, writing a note, removing it — happens on your Discogs wantlist
too, and anything you do on discogs.com shows up here. There is one list, in two
places.

## Before you start: link your Discogs account

The wishlist works only when your Discogs account is linked to Vinylmania — the
same link the library uses. You set it up once from your profile.

If your account isn't linked, opening **My wishlist** shows a "Link your Discogs
account" message with a button to your profile instead of a list. Follow it,
link your account, then come back.

If you once linked Discogs but later removed Vinylmania's access from your
Discogs settings, you'll see "Your Discogs link is no longer valid" instead.
Re-link from your profile to continue.

## Finding your wishlist

Open **My wishlist** from the top navigation. It sits right next to **My
library**, on desktop and in the mobile menu.

The page lists your wantlist as record cards, in the same style as your library
and search results. Long wishlists are split into pages — use **Previous** and
**Next** at the bottom.

If your Discogs wantlist is empty, you'll see a short "nothing here yet" message,
not an error.

## Adding a record

You can add a record to your wishlist from two places:

- **Search results** — each result has two actions. One adds the record to your
  library; the other, marked with a heart, adds it to your wishlist. They're
  separate on purpose: adding to your wishlist never touches your library.
- **A release's detail page** — open any release and use the **Add to wishlist**
  button, next to **Add to library**.

Either way, the record is written straight to your Discogs wantlist. Once it's
added, the action shows it's already there ("In your wishlist" / "Added to
wishlist") and won't add a second copy.

**Adding a record you already own is allowed.** Vinylmania adds it to your
wishlist and tells you it's already in your library — it does not remove it from
your library. Only the reverse happens automatically (see
[Buying a record](#buying-a-record)).

## The rating on the card vs. your personal rating

Every card shows a rating badge. That badge is the **Discogs community rating**
for the release — the average of what all Discogs users think of it. It means
exactly the same thing here as it does on your library cards and in search
results.

Your own **personal rating** for a wishlist record is a separate thing. You set
it on the release's detail page, described next. It never changes the badge on
the card.

## Adding a note and a personal rating

Discogs keeps two things for each wantlist entry: a personal rating and a note.
You can edit both in Vinylmania.

1. Open the record's detail page (tap its card in your wishlist).
2. Find the **Your wishlist notes** panel. It appears only for records that are
   in your wishlist.
3. Set a **personal rating** by tapping the stars (0 to 5), the same star
   control used elsewhere in the app.
4. Type why you want the record in the **Notes** field.

There is no Save button. Your rating saves the moment you tap a star, and your
note saves when you click away from the field or press Enter. If a save doesn't
go through, you'll see a message with a "Try again" option — the value isn't
silently treated as saved.

Clearing a note and confirming saves an empty note (it removes the annotation),
so it's a real change, not a no-op.

## Removing a record

When you no longer want a record, remove it from the **My wishlist** page:

1. On the record's card, choose **Remove from wishlist** (the trash icon).
2. A short "Remove from wishlist?" confirmation appears. Choose **Remove** to
   confirm, or **Cancel** to keep it.

Confirming removes the record from your Discogs wantlist as well, and it
disappears from the list. If the removal can't reach Discogs, the record stays
put and you can try again.

## Buying a record

When you buy a wishlist record and add it to your library, Vinylmania
automatically removes it from your wishlist — the same thing discogs.com does.
A record is never in both your library and your wishlist at once.

If the library add works but the wishlist removal fails, you'll see a note like:
"Added to your library. We couldn't remove it from your wishlist — remove it
there when you can." Add to your library succeeded; just remove the leftover
wishlist entry yourself when convenient.

## How syncing works

- **Changes you make in Vinylmania** — adding, removing, editing a note or
  rating — show up right away, both here and on discogs.com.
- **Changes you make on discogs.com** — Vinylmania picks them up within about
  five minutes, or immediately if you use the **Refresh** button on the
  **My wishlist** page.

There is no background syncing. Vinylmania checks your Discogs wantlist when you
open the page (at most once every few minutes) and when you press Refresh.

## Not included yet

These are planned for a later version and aren't available now:

- Price or marketplace alerts when a wanted record goes on sale
- Folders or categories within the wishlist
- Reordering the wishlist by hand
- Push notifications
